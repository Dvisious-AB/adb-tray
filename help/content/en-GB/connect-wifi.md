You use the device without a cable. It takes a couple of minutes the first time. The device and the computer must be on the same network.

## Switch from USB {#from-usb}

1. Connect the device by cable. See [Connect a device by USB](help:connect-usb).
2. Point to the device in the menu and click :button[device.switchToWifi]{icon=wifi-high}.
3. Unplug the cable. The device stays in the menu with (Wi-Fi).

This lasts until the device restarts.

## Pair without a cable {#pair}

4. Turn on :term[wireless debugging] under **Developer options** on the device.
5. Choose **Pair device with pairing code** on the device.
6. Click :button[menu.connectWifi], then :button[menu.pair] in the menu.
7. Type the pairing address and the code the device shows. Click :button[dialog.ok].

## Connect to an address you have used {#saved-address}

8. Point to :button[menu.connectWifi]. The addresses you have used are at the top.
9. Click the address.

## If it does not work {#if-it-fails}

If the connection fails, the device has probably restarted. Switch from USB again, or pair again.
