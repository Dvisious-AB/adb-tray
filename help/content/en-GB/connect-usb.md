You prepare the device once. After that it appears in the menu whenever you plug in the cable. It takes about five minutes.

## Unlock the developer options {#developer-options}

1. Open **Settings** on the device.
2. Choose **About device**, then **Software information**.
3. Tap **Build number** seven times.

The names can differ slightly between manufacturers.

## Turn on USB debugging {#usb-debugging}

4. Go back to **Settings** and open **Developer options**.
5. Turn on :term[USB debugging].

## Plug in the cable {#plug-in}

6. Connect the device to the computer with a USB cable.
7. Tap **Allow** in the dialog on the device. Ticking **Always allow from this computer** saves time.
8. Click the icon in the menu bar. The device appears with its model name and (USB).

## If it does not work {#if-it-fails}

If the device is shown with :button[device.unauthorized]: tap **Allow** on the device.

If the device does not appear at all: try another cable. Some cables can only charge.
