You decide how the device's screen should stand, also while mirroring. It takes a few seconds.

## Lock the screen {#lock}

1. Point to the device in the menu.
2. Click :button[device.lockPortrait] or :button[device.lockLandscape].

## Let the screen rotate by itself {#automatic}

3. Point to the device in the menu.
4. Click :button[device.autoRotate].
